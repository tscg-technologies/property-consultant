Strategic Perspective

Objective Real Estate Investment Advisory

Real estate can serve as an effective wealth preservation and cash flow vehicle when analyzed with analytical discipline. However, speculative marketing often exaggerates returns while hiding holding costs, illiquidity, and tenancy vacancies.

At Property Consultant, we do not sell get-rich-quick property schemes or promise assured returns. Instead, we assist investors in evaluating cash flow durability, regulatory zoning, micro-market supply pipelines, and exit liquidity.

Asset Spectrum

Investment Horizons & Asset Classes

Comparing residential rentals, commercial assets, and strategic land investments across risk-return parameters.

Residential Properties

Stable asset with widespread tenant demand and lower capital threshold. Rental yields typically range between 2.5% to 3.8% annually in prime metros (illustrative benchmark).

  • High liquidity relative to other property
  • Consistent end-user tenant pool
  • Easier mortgage accessibility

Commercial Offices & Retail

Higher yield potential with long-term corporate leases and built-in escalation clauses. Illustrative yields historically hover between 6.5% to 8.5% (illustrative demo metrics).

  • 3 to 9-year institutional leases
  • Grade-A corporate tenant profiles
  • Triple-net lease frameworks

Plotted Land Developments

Zero depreciation on structural materials with potential capital appreciation along major planned expressway corridors and transit hubs.

  • Zero building aging or upkeep cost
  • Complete developmental flexibility
  • Long-term family wealth foundation
Risk Mitigation

Critical Real Estate Evaluation Factors

The fundamental metrics every investor should assess before deploying capital into property.

1. Location & Infrastructure

Upcoming metro stations, road widening, business parks, and arterial flyovers driving sustainable tenant demand.

2. Net Yield vs Gross Yield

Factoring property taxes, vacancy allowances, society maintenance dues, and annual repair reserves into actual returns.

3. Developer Execution Track

Historical delivery timelines, litigation frequency, and construction quality audits across past completed projects.

4. Exit Horizon & Liquidity

Understanding secondary market resale velocity and typical time-to-liquidate before entering any illiquid asset.
