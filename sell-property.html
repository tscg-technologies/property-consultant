Required Documents

Essential Property Records for Selling

Having clear documentation ready drastically shortens negotiation cycles and prevents legal friction during buyer due diligence.

Title Deed & Chain of Ownership

Registered Sale Deed, Conveyance Deed, or Gift Deed establishing 30-year unencumbered title continuity.

Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

Updated EC demonstrating that the property is free from mortgages, pending court attachments, or liens.

Approved Sanction Plan & Khata / Tax Receipts

Municipal-approved layout drawings, completion certificate (OC where applicable), and latest property tax receipts.

Advisory Transparency: Property Consultant provides marketing presentation and buyer coordination. We do not provide statutory legal warranties, nor do we guarantee specific closing timelines or prices.
